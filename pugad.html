Pugo Adventure (PUGAD)

Pugo Adventure, commonly known as PUGAD, is a premier adventure destination nestled in the mountains of Pugo, La Union.

Key Attractions:

  • Longest Ziplines in the region
  • ATV Adventure Tracks
  • Wall Climbing and Rappelling
  • Multiple Swimming Pools and Picnic Huts
