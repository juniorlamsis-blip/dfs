Kultura Splash Wave

Kultura Splash Wave is a top-tier water park and resort located in Pugo, La Union, designed for families and large groups.

The resort features world-class wave pools, giant slides, and comfortable cottages that blend modern recreation with traditional Filipino aesthetics.
