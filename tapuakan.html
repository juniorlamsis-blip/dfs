Tapuakan River

The Cleanest Inland Body of Water in the Region

Tapuakan River is often referred to as the "Cleanest Inland Body of Water" in the region, offering a serene escape into nature.

The river is famous for its crystal-clear, cold spring water that flows directly from the pristine mountains of the Cordilleras. It is a sacred natural landmark for the locals, providing perfect spots for picnics and soul-soothing relaxation.
